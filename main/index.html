AniList v1.0.3

2026-10-12 — Sign-in works

  • **Fixes AniList sign-in failing with "unsupported grant type".** The request sent redirect_uri, which AniList's implicit grant does not take; it now sends only client_id and response_type and AniList redirects to the client's registered URL. Mana still closes the sign-in sheet on the com.seyden.mana callback scheme. Confirmed working in Mana.

2026-10-12 — Sign-in diagnostics

  • New **Troubleshooting → Show Sign-in Request** setting. While it is on, signing in opens an echo page instead of AniList, showing the exact URL and parameters Mana opened, to pin down why AniList answers "unsupported grant type".

2026-10-12 — Sign-in fix

  • AniList sign-in failed with "unsupported grant type", meaning AniList got no usable response_type=token/client_id. The parameters were only in the request's params; they are now in the authorize URL itself as well, in case Mana does not forward params. This did not fix it on its own.

2026-10-12

  • Initial implementation: an AniList ContentTracker for manga, manhwa, manhua and light novels.
  • Sign-in through AniList's OAuth implicit grant in Mana's web authentication sheet, using the bundled client 53586 (redirect URL com.seyden.mana://oauth/anilist). Your own client ID can be set in settings, or an access token pasted there instead.
  • Reading progress syncs as chapters are read: the first chapter moves a title to Reading, the last one marks it Completed (counting a reread when it was being reread), and empty start/finish dates are filled in. Both behaviours can be turned off.
  • Entry editor covering status, score in the account's own scoring format, chapter and volume progress, rereads, dates, notes, custom lists and privacy.
  • Discover page with five sections loaded in a single request, your AniList lists in the library, search with genre/tag include-exclude, format, status, country, year and "on my list" filters, eight sort orders, and anilist.co/manga/<id> deep links.
  • Search filters are checked before a search runs: a tag both included and excluded, a year out of range, or "on my list" while signed out is reported on the field instead of returning nothing.
  • Result tiles show format, status, chapters, volumes, score and year, plus a badge with your list status when the title is already on your AniList.
  • Title pages carry characters, staff, relations, recommendations, external links and the AniList/MyAnimeList ids for linking sources. Characters, staff, relations and recommendations show "view more" when there are more than ten.
  • Manhwa and manhua recommend the webtoon reader; manga recommend paged right-to-left.
